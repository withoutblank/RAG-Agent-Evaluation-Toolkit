## Interpretation

- Highest Hit Rate@k: **large-k3, large-k5, small-k3, small-k5** at **1.000** (ties retained).
- Required-fact coverage averaged **0.396** for 300-character chunks and **0.385** for 600-character chunks. This is an association between presets, not an isolated chunk-size effect.
- Moving from top-k=3 to top-k=5 changed mean Source Recall@k from **0.948** to **0.990**; citation precision changed from 0.906 to 0.906.

No composite winner is calculated. Comparisons above use each named metric directly. Chunk size and overlap change together, so differences cannot be attributed to chunk size alone.
