# Limited Warranty Policy

> Fictional sample data: these terms are invented for evaluation and do not
> represent any real manufacturer, vehicle, warranty, or consumer-law advice.

## Coverage periods

The new-vehicle limited warranty lasts for four years or 80,000 kilometres from
the original delivery date, whichever occurs first. It covers faults in supplied
materials or workmanship when the vehicle has been used normally. A repair or
replacement under warranty does not restart or extend the original coverage
period.

The high-voltage battery and drive unit have separate coverage for eight years or
160,000 kilometres, whichever occurs first. During that period, the battery
capacity provision applies if an authorised diagnostic measures retained usable
capacity below 70 percent of the service baseline. Displayed range, a third-party
scan, or one unusually short journey is not a capacity measurement. Normal
capacity loss that remains at or above 70 percent is not a defect.

Parts installed during a covered repair receive the remainder of the original
warranty or 12 months from installation, whichever is longer. The warranty
transfers to a later private owner without a fee, provided the vehicle identity
and delivery record can be verified.

## What is and is not covered

Covered examples include a failed onboard charger, a defective factory sensor,
or a battery module fault confirmed by diagnostics. Software supplied with the
vehicle is covered when a defect prevents a documented vehicle function and
cannot be corrected through the normal update process.

The warranty does not cover collision damage, flood or fire damage from an
external source, misuse, racing, unauthorised modification, improper towing,
commercial overloading, or damage caused by incompatible charging equipment.
Damage caused by an extension lead or an unearthed outlet is excluded. Cosmetic
wear, tyre wear, brake friction-material wear, wiper blades, cabin filters, and
gradual battery ageing above the capacity threshold are normal maintenance or
wear items.

Missing a scheduled inspection does not automatically cancel the entire
warranty. However, a claim may be declined when failure to maintain the vehicle
caused or materially worsened the reported fault. Owners may use a qualified
independent repairer, but they should retain itemised records and ensure the
specified fluids, parts, and procedures are used.

## Making a claim

The owner should report the symptom promptly through the support portal or an
authorised service location. Include the vehicle identifier, current odometer,
delivery date if known, warning messages, relevant photos, and maintenance
records. Support may request remote diagnostics, but the owner can arrange an
in-person inspection instead. An estimate for non-covered work must be approved
before that work begins.

Warranty decisions are based on inspection and the terms in effect on the
original delivery date. The service team will explain the covered repair,
excluded work, and escalation route in writing. Rights that cannot lawfully be
excluded by the customer's jurisdiction remain unaffected by this fictional
limited warranty.

## Related services

Roadside assistance is a convenience program, not an extension of warranty
coverage. Its four-year eligibility period happens to match the new-vehicle
warranty at first delivery, but an incident can be eligible for roadside help
without being a warrantable defect. Scheduled maintenance, charging fees,
replacement tyres, insurance, registration, and transport after an accident are
not included unless another written program explicitly says so.

Over-the-air software updates do not change the original warranty start date.
Installing a normal signed update does not void coverage. Bypassing security
controls, installing unsigned software, or altering safety systems may exclude a
claim when the alteration caused the problem.
