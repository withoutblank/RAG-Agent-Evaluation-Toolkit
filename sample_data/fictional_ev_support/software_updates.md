# Vehicle Software Updates

> Fictional sample data: the software, update service, and policies in this
> document do not belong to a real vehicle or manufacturer.

## Update types

The vehicle receives signed software packages over the air. Feature updates add
or refine user-facing behaviour and can normally be scheduled by the driver.
Safety or security updates correct higher-priority issues and may present a
shorter installation window. Maps and entertainment applications can update
separately without changing the core vehicle-control software.

Release notes appear in the vehicle and support portal before installation. They
identify the software version, affected functions, approximate installation
time, and whether settings may change. A release note may describe improved range
estimation; that can change the displayed estimate without changing the
battery's physical capacity.

## Preparing to install

To start a core update, the vehicle must be in Park, connected to a reliable
network, and have at least 30 percent traction-battery charge. Charging may
continue during download, but the connector should not be removed after
installation begins. Close all doors and windows, remove occupants and pets, and
allow the displayed installation time plus 15 minutes before the next planned
trip.

The vehicle cannot be driven, fast charged, or used for climate preconditioning
during the installation stage. Phone-key access may be temporarily unavailable,
so keep the physical backup key accessible. Fleet administrators can define an
installation window, but the driver receives an in-vehicle warning before the
vehicle becomes unavailable.

## Interruptions and failures

Losing network access during download pauses and later resumes the download.
Power loss during installation is handled by a protected recovery partition; do
not disconnect the 12-volt battery or attempt to reinstall the package manually.
If the progress display does not change for 90 minutes beyond the stated
installation time, record the version and message, then contact support.

After a failed installation, the vehicle retains or restores the last verified
version when possible. A persistent red update warning, unavailable safety
function, or vehicle that will not select Drive requires support. If the vehicle
is unsafe to move, roadside assistance can transport it under the separate
roadside terms.

## Settings, data, and warranty

Most driver profiles, charging targets, paired keys, and accessibility settings
are retained. Release notes explicitly identify a reset. Before a major update,
drivers may export supported settings from the portal. The update system reports
software version, download status, installation result, and diagnostic fault
codes. It does not upload cabin audio for the update process.

Drivers can postpone ordinary feature updates for up to 30 days. A safety or
security update may become mandatory after the date shown in its notice.
Disabling telemetry does not block delivery of critical update packages or
receipt of an installation result because those functions are necessary for
vehicle safety and service integrity.

Installing an official signed update does not void the limited warranty and does
not change its start date. Unsigned packages, bypassed security controls, or
altered safety systems are unsupported. A warranty claim may be excluded only
when an unauthorised alteration caused or materially worsened the fault.

## Good update practice

Review release notes, confirm the charge level, and schedule installation for a
time when the vehicle is not needed. After installation, verify the displayed
version and check charging, keys, driver-assistance settings, and accessibility
preferences before travel. Report unexpected behaviour with the exact version
and time rather than repeatedly restarting the vehicle.
