# Vehicle Delivery Process

> Fictional sample data: this delivery checklist, timing, and policy are invented
> and do not describe a real seller or vehicle.

## Before the appointment

The delivery team sends a proposed two-hour appointment window after the vehicle
passes its preparation inspection. At least 24 hours before that window, the
named recipient must confirm identity details, delivery address, payment status,
registration instructions, and any accessibility needs. Evidence of insurance is
required only where the fictional delivery jurisdiction requires it.

To reschedule without a handling review, use the support portal at least 24 hours
before the start of the appointment window. A late change may be necessary for
weather, transport interruption, an incomplete payment, or a failed quality
check. The team provides a new window; it does not promise a particular driver or
carrier.

Only the named recipient or a previously approved representative may accept the
vehicle. The representative must show their own identity document and the
one-time authorisation code. Support staff never ask for an account password or
full payment-card number at handover.

## Preparation standard

Before dispatch, the team checks the vehicle identifier, exterior and interior
condition, tyre pressures, lights, charging inlet, supplied cable, backup key,
software status, and outstanding safety notices. The traction battery target is
at least 70 percent on arrival unless transport or local safety rules require a
lower level. A portable cable is included only when it appears on the signed
order; a home charging station and installation are not automatically included.

The vehicle arrives with a digital quick-start guide, limited-warranty summary,
maintenance schedule, roadside instructions, privacy notice, and an item list.
Registration documents may be electronic or may follow separately according to
local processing rules.

## Handover inspection

Before accepting, compare the displayed vehicle identifier with the delivery
record. Walk around the vehicle in good light and check glass, panels, wheels,
tyres, seats, screens, cables, and keys. Confirm the displayed charge level and
odometer, pair one owner key, adjust accessibility settings, and locate the
in-vehicle Help menu. Do not sign a blank inspection record.

Record any visible difference on the handover form and take a clear photo before
the carrier leaves. A safety concern, wrong vehicle identifier, or major
undisclosed damage is grounds to pause acceptance while support reviews the
delivery. Minor cosmetic items can be recorded for a later appointment without
preventing safe use.

## After handover

The recipient receives a copy of the signed condition record in the support
portal. Report a delivery-condition issue that could not reasonably be seen at
handover within 48 hours, including photos and the odometer. The 48-hour process
records delivery condition; it does not shorten any non-excludable right or the
separate limited-warranty period.

The new-vehicle warranty and roadside eligibility begin on the original delivery
date. Scheduled maintenance is due at 12 months or 20,000 kilometres, whichever
comes first. The routine charging target is 80 percent, despite the delivery
preparation target of at least 70 percent.

Before the first trip, review charging safety, set a suitable charge limit, check
tyre pressure, and install any offered safety update. Questions about registration
fees, insurance premiums, tax credits, resale value, or home-electrical work are
outside this fictional delivery guide.
