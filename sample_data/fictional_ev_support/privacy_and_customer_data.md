# Privacy and Customer Data

> Fictional sample data: this notice describes an invented data service. It is
> not a real privacy policy and is not legal advice.

## Data categories and purposes

The support system processes account details, vehicle identifiers, service and
warranty records, charging summaries, software version, diagnostic fault codes,
and support conversations. It uses these records to provide requested services,
maintain safety and security, diagnose faults, administer warranty and roadside
programs, and improve de-identified reliability statistics.

Precise location is used when the driver starts navigation, explicitly shares a
roadside location, locates their parked vehicle, or enables optional fleet
location history. Continuous location history is off by default for ordinary
owner accounts. The update process records version, download state, installation
result, and fault codes; it does not collect cabin audio.

Voice commands are processed as short command clips only when voice activation is
enabled. A driver can choose on-device command processing where available. Raw
clips sent for online processing are deleted within 30 days unless the user
separately submits a clip with a support case. Cabin-camera images used for local
driver-attention features are not uploaded during normal operation.

## Choices and necessary data

Owners can disable optional analytics, continuous location history, and
personalised feature suggestions in Privacy Settings. Disabling optional
telemetry does not stop safety diagnostics, theft protection requested by the
owner, critical software delivery, odometer records needed for warranty, or data
required to complete an active roadside request.

A roadside request shares the confirmed pickup location with the assigned
provider until the incident closes. The status remains in the support record, but
the provider does not receive the owner's complete account history. Charging
exports show energy and time; a home location is masked unless the owner
explicitly includes it.

## Retention and security

Ordinary account and vehicle-assignment records are kept while the account is
active. Service, safety, warranty, and odometer records are retained for the
coverage period plus seven years so a later claim or safety review can be
audited. Roadside incident details are retained for three years. Optional precise
location history is retained for 90 days. De-identified aggregate reliability
statistics may be retained without an account identifier.

Access is role-based, administrative access uses multi-factor authentication, and
exports are encrypted in transit. A portal export link expires after 24 hours.
The support team will never request an account password, backup-key code, or full
payment-card number in a message.

## Access, correction, deletion, and export

An account holder can request a machine-readable export or ask to access,
correct, or delete personal data through the Privacy section of the support
portal. The privacy team acknowledges a request within 10 business days and
normally completes it within 30 days after identity verification. A complex
request may take up to 60 days; the team explains the extension.

Deletion removes optional profile and analytics data that is no longer needed. It
does not erase records that must be retained for safety, fraud prevention,
warranty administration, an active dispute, or another legal obligation. The
response identifies retained categories and their reason rather than claiming
that every record was erased.

## Fleet accounts and transfers

For a fleet account, the organisation decides which drivers are assigned and
whether optional operational location is enabled. The fictional support operator
secures the portal and follows account instructions, while the organisation must
notify its drivers and manage lawful access. Drivers can question an incorrect
assignment through either the organisation or the privacy portal.

Before selling or returning a vehicle, remove personal destinations, paired
phones, garage controls, and digital keys, then perform the ownership-transfer
reset. The reset removes local profile data but does not delete service or
warranty records. The next owner receives no access to the previous owner's
support conversations or precise location history.

Suspected account compromise should be reported through the portal's Security
section. Change the password, revoke unfamiliar sessions, and review digital
keys. A vehicle safety emergency or exposed high-voltage component belongs with
emergency or roadside support, not a privacy request.
