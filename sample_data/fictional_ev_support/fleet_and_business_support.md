# Fleet and Business Support

> Fictional sample data: this business program and its portal are invented for
> evaluation. They do not describe a real fleet product or commercial offer.

## Account eligibility

An organisation operating five or more supported vehicles can request a fleet
account. Approval requires an authorised business contact, billing details, the
vehicle identifiers to enrol, and confirmation that drivers have received the
organisation's privacy notice. Smaller groups use ordinary owner accounts but may
still export records vehicle by vehicle.

Fleet enrolment does not change the vehicle's limited warranty, roadside
eligibility, scheduled-maintenance intervals, or charging safety instructions.
Each period continues from that vehicle's original delivery date and odometer.
The fleet portal coordinates information; it does not create extra mechanical
coverage unless a separate signed service plan is listed.

## Roles and access

The portal provides three roles. Account administrators manage users, billing,
privacy settings, and vehicle assignment. Fleet managers view operational
status, charging summaries, maintenance due dates, and approved location data.
Drivers see only vehicles assigned to them, their own charging sessions, and
support requests. A user should receive the least-privileged role needed.

Administrators must review access every three months and remove a departing user
within one business day. Multi-factor authentication is required for
administrators and fleet managers. The audit log records sign-in, role change,
vehicle assignment, export, and deletion-request actions for 24 months.

## Charging and maintenance operations

Charging-session exports include vehicle identifier, charger category, start and
end time, energy delivered, and cost entered by the organisation. Home addresses
are masked in standard manager views. A driver can submit a home-charging
reimbursement rate, but payroll, tax treatment, and electricity contracts remain
the organisation's responsibility.

Maintenance alerts use the standard 12-month or 20,000-kilometre interval. Fleet
managers can group appointments and export service records, but they cannot reset
a maintenance reminder before work is completed. Severe-use vehicles may need
the separate six-month or 10,000-kilometre intermediate inspection described in
the maintenance guide.

Roadside requests use the standard in-vehicle Help menu or support portal. The
fleet manager can view request status when the organisation has enabled
operational location sharing. Collision transport, charging fees, replacement
parts, and additional towing distance remain excluded under the ordinary terms.

## Data exports and privacy

The organisation is responsible for telling drivers which optional operational
data it enables and why. Drivers must be given a route to question inaccurate
assignment or location records. Safety diagnostics necessary to operate and
repair the vehicle are separate from optional fleet analytics.

Administrators can export charging, odometer, maintenance, energy-use, and
roadside-event records as CSV. Location history is disabled by default and, when
enabled, is retained for 90 days. Aggregated energy summaries without a precise
route are retained for 24 months. Export links expire after 24 hours and should
not be forwarded.

When a vehicle leaves the fleet, the administrator must unassign drivers, export
any records the organisation must retain, and remove the vehicle from the
account. Removal stops new fleet analytics but does not erase safety, warranty,
or legally required service records. The privacy guide explains access,
correction, deletion, and the division of responsibility between a fleet
organisation and the fictional support operator.
