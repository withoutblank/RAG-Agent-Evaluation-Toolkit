# Roadside Assistance

> Fictional sample data: this assistance program, its service area, and all
> procedures are invented and are not offered by a real organisation.

## Eligibility and access

Roadside assistance is available 24 hours a day during the first four years from
the original vehicle delivery date. There is no kilometre limit during that
period. Eligibility follows the vehicle when ownership changes, but it does not
restart for the next owner. Help can be requested through the in-vehicle **Help >
Roadside** menu or the support portal at `support.example.invalid`.

The driver should provide the vehicle identifier, exact location, a safe callback
method, number of occupants, warning messages, and a short description of the
incident. Location sharing begins only after the driver confirms the request.
When the vehicle is in a dangerous traffic position or anyone is injured, contact
local emergency services before using the support program.

## Included incidents

The program includes:

- a jump start for the separate 12-volt battery;
- fitting the vehicle's usable spare wheel, when supplied;
- tyre inflation or transport when no usable spare is available;
- lockout assistance after reasonable digital-key recovery steps;
- transport after a traction-battery depletion;
- transport for a vehicle fault that makes continued driving unsafe.

For a depleted traction battery, the normal destination is the nearest compatible
public charger or authorised service location, chosen according to safe access
and provider capability. A mobile energy top-up may be offered in some areas but
is not guaranteed. Drivers must not push-charge, tow-charge, or jump-start the
high-voltage battery.

For an eligible mechanical or electrical breakdown, transport is provided to the
nearest authorised service location within 100 kilometres of the pickup point.
The driver may request another destination and pay the additional distance.
Passengers and mobility equipment are accommodated when the driver describes
those needs during the request, although a separate accessible vehicle may take
longer to arrive.

## Service area and exclusions

Service is provided on publicly accessible roads and car parks in the supported
delivery region. A vehicle more than 20 metres from a formed, safely accessible
road may require a specialist recovery provider at the driver's expense.
Ferries, restricted mines, competition venues, closed roads, and locations that
the responder judges unsafe are outside standard service.

The program does not include fuel for another vehicle, charging fees, replacement
tyres, replacement keys, parts, repair labour, accommodation, or transport after
a collision. It also excludes bog recovery, flood recovery, repeated deliberate
depletion, commercial overloading, and incidents caused by unauthorised
modification. The responder may still coordinate help and provide a price before
non-covered work begins.

## Waiting safely

Move out of traffic if the vehicle can be moved safely, select Park, turn on
hazard lights, and wait behind a safety barrier when local guidance says it is
safer than remaining in the vehicle. Do not touch exposed cables, orange
high-voltage components, leaking fluid, or a smoking battery enclosure. Tell the
dispatcher immediately about fire, floodwater, collision damage, unusual heat,
or chemical odour.

An estimated arrival time is not a guarantee because weather, traffic, distance,
and responder availability vary. The portal shows status updates without
revealing the responder's private contact details. Roadside assistance is
separate from warranty: transport may be included even when the repair itself is
not covered.
