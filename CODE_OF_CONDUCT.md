# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participate with respect, patience, and technical good faith. Welcome
questions, give actionable feedback, credit other people's work, and disagree
with ideas without attacking people.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized attention, deliberate
intimidation, and publication of another person's private information are not
acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This standard applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, and
other spaces where someone represents the project. Maintainers may edit,
remove, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict
participation when conduct is harmful.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's GitHub
profile. Do not include sensitive details in a public issue. Reports will be
reviewed fairly and with as much confidentiality as practical.
